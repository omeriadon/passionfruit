    • An eSIM is a digital SIM that eliminates the need for a physical SIM card. With eSIM, you can quickly and easily transfer an existing carrier plan or get a new carrier plan, all digitally. You can even store multiple eSIMs on the same device and use two phone numbers at the same time. And it’s more secure — someone can’t remove the physical SIM card if your iPhone is lost or stolen. In just a few simple steps, you are ready to make calls, send messages and browse the web. Learn more about eSIM (Opens in a new window) .

      iPhone Duo is activated with an eSIM and does not support a physical SIM. Use of an eSIM requires a carrier that supports eSIM and a carrier plan. See your carrier for details. Learn more about eSIM and carriers (Opens in a new window) .

    • iPhone Duo doesn’t have a physical SIM tray and activates only using eSIM. Use of an eSIM requires a carrier that supports eSIM and a wireless service plan. With eSIM Carrier Activation, supported carriers can assign an eSIM to your iPhone digitally. If your carrier supports eSIM Quick Transfer, you can transfer the SIM from your previous iPhone to your new iPhone without contacting your carrier. Additionally, your carrier can provide you with a QR code or ask you to use their carrier app to activate an eSIM. Learn more about setting up your eSIM (Opens in a new window).

      While travelling internationally, your network provider may offer convenient and affordable international roaming options. Additionally, you can use a travel eSIM at your destination. Learn more about using eSIM while travelling (Opens in a new window).

    • An unconnected iPhone purchased from the Apple website is unlocked. This means you aren’t tied to a single network provider or a multi-year service contract. You can choose the network and plan that are right for you. Once your new iPhone is activated, it remains unlocked, which means you can use it with any network that provides service for iPhone.

    • All iPhone models are world phones, so you can use them almost anywhere. Whether you are a GSM or CDMA network customer, you can roam internationally on GSM networks in over 200 countries or regions around the world. If you financed your iPhone with your service provider, contact them to verify that you can roam internationally.

      Availability of 5G and LTE data varies in some countries or regions and depends on carrier support for certain 5G and LTE radio frequencies. Contact your service provider for more information regarding international roaming policies and rates.
